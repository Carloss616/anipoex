# Metrics

Figures computed on the device from AniList data, each in one pure function with
`bun test` coverage.

* [List counts](list-counts.md) - Entries per list status, null until loaded, custom lists excluded.
* [Reading progress](reading-progress.md) - The `c.12/40` caption, its spoken form and the bar fraction.
* [Unread chapters](unread-chapters.md) - The `+N` a source shows: whole chapters beyond the user's progress.
